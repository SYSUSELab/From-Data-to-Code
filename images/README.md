# Project images

`from-data-to-code-hero-light.png` and `from-data-to-code-hero-dark.png` are matching 1672 x 941 promotional overview illustrations for the website. They were created with the built-in image generation tool, using the repository's original bridge-themed hero as a visual reference. The dark variant retains the light variant's content and layout.

The hero summarizes 114 included papers, 13 training-data quality headings, 12 relationship families, eight generated-code quality dimensions, and 101 relationship assignments from 67 contributing papers. The left-side examples are illustrative, not the complete list of thirteen headings. Ribbon widths and the bridge geometry do not encode measured weights or causal strength. Refer to the released evidence tables for the research results.

The six taxonomy/method SVGs are rendered by `scripts/render_overviews.py` from the released tables. Their PNG counterparts support repository previews. `paper_collection.png`, `lifecycle.png`, and `sankey.png` are rendered from the revision's manuscript PDF figures; `sankey.pdf` preserves the manuscript graph as a vector figure.
